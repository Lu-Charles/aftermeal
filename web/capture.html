Before your first serving
  1. Use a kitchen scale and record its smallest increment, such as 1 g or 0.1 g.
  2. Weigh the empty, dry plate. Keep the same plate and leave cutlery off the scale.
  3. Add food. Record the total plate + food weight, then photograph the whole plate from a fixed angle.
  4. After eating or removing some food, weigh and photograph it again. You can record several stages of the same serving.

Include full portions, small leftovers and residue in the pilot. Take each photo at the same angle and distance. A serving with more stages still counts as one serving.

Photos and readings are saved on this computer. Analysis uploads in the Workspace are separate. Export a copy to back up this collection.
