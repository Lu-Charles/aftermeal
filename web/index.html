    What do these numbers mean?+

    These are average errors in percentage points. Predicting 50% when 40% remains gives an error of 10 points. Lower is better; 9.63 points does not mean 90.37% accuracy.

    Evaluation method+

    Five held-out folds per dataset, with 50 calibration pairs per fit. Food categories or participants and reviewed similar images stay separate across fitting boundaries. Model selection uses inner validation; errors are averaged within groups, then across groups.

    One calibration repetition. These results measure within-dataset calibration, not transfer to new settings.

    About Aftermeal

    Food-leftover estimation from paired photos.

    What to expect

    This is an experimental estimate, not a weight measurement. Residue, lighting and unfamiliar foods can cause large errors. Highlights do not determine the percentage.
